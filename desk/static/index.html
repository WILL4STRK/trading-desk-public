How to import
thinkorswim desktop app
  1. Press Copy for thinkorswim.
  2. In the left sidebar's Watchlist, open the watchlist menu and choose Import.
  3. Set Load from to Paste symbols from clipboard, then Save.
thinkorswim Web

It has no list import. Click New Watchlist, give it a name, then add the tickers one at a time.

TradingView
  1. Upload list needs a paid TradingView plan. On a paid plan: press Download for TradingView, then in TradingView click Watchlist (top of the right panel) and choose Upload list…, and pick the file from Downloads.
  2. On the free plan: use the Chart in TradingView links, or add tickers one at a time with the watchlist's + button.

TradingView and thinkorswim Web were checked on 2026-11-10. The thinkorswim desktop steps come from its help pages.

Practice account

In paper mode, auto paper can trade ready signals by itself in a pretend practice account kept in the desk's data folder. It never talks to a broker. Practice orders fill from free delayed prices, so they are for practising the steps, not for measuring results.

Trading rules

Limits are percentages of your account, so they grow and shrink with it.

Short selling: rules and risks

This desk reads free, delayed public data and does arithmetic. It never places a real order: you place each one at your broker yourself. It is for education and is not financial advice. Most day traders lose money. A stop is a plan: in a fast market it can fill worse than its price. Check every number against your broker before you send an order.
